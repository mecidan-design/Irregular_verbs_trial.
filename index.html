5th Form · English

Past Simple Quest

Irregular verbs, group by group. Five chapters of games and exercises that check your answers for you.

How to use this

Do Chapters I to IV in order, one for each group you study in class. Leave the Final Trial for the end: it mixes all ten groups, like the real test. Nothing is saved, so you can play as many times as you want.
